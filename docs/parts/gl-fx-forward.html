Spot date
The standard settlement date for FX, usually two business days after the trade.
Covered interest parity
The no-arbitrage link between spot, forward and the two currencies' interest rates.
Forward points
Forward minus spot, quoted in pips (× 10,000, or × 100 for JPY pairs).
NDF
Non-deliverable forward: only the difference between forward and fixing is paid, usually in USD.
Fixing
The official reference rate used to settle an NDF or option, such as TAIFX1 or PTAX.
FX swap
A spot or near-date exchange plus the opposite forward exchange.
