Breakeven inflation
The fixed rate of a new zero-coupon inflation swap; the inflation rate at which nominal and inflation-linked bonds return the same.
Index lag
The delay between a swap date and the month of the price index it references (2 months for UK RPI swaps, 3 months for US CPI and euro HICPx).
Base index
The lagged index level at the start of the swap, against which the final index is compared.
Seasonality
The regular monthly pattern in price indices that the forward index curve must include.
IE01
PV change for a 1bp rise in the breakeven inflation curve.
