Structured product
A note combining a bond with options or other derivatives into one security with a tailored payoff.
Capital protection
The guaranteed share of the notional repaid at maturity, as long as the issuer does not default.
Participation
The share of the underlying's rise paid out by a note.
Autocallable
A note that repays early with a coupon if the underlying is above a trigger on an observation date.
Down-and-in put
A put that only comes alive if the underlying falls through a barrier during its life.
SimpleFlow
Booking record for a single known cash payment; PV = ± amount × discount factor.
