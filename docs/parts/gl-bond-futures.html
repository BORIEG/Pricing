Cheapest-to-deliver (CTD)
The bond in a futures delivery basket that is cheapest for the seller to deliver; the one with the highest implied repo.
Conversion factor
Exchange-set multiplier (price per 1 face at a 6% yield) that scales the invoice price for each deliverable bond.
Invoice price
Amount the buyer pays at delivery: futures price × conversion factor + accrued interest.
Gross basis
Bond clean price minus futures price × conversion factor.
Net basis
Gross basis minus carry; approximately the value of the seller's delivery options.
Implied repo
Financing rate earned by buying the bond, selling the future and delivering.
Delivery options
The seller's choices of which bond and which day to deliver, which lower the futures price.
