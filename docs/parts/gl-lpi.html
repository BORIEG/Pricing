LPI (Limited Price Indexation)
UK pension indexation where each year's inflation is clipped into a band, typically [0%, 5%], before compounding.
YoY inflation
Year-on-year inflation: the index this year divided by the index a year earlier, minus 1.
YoY caplet / floorlet
An option paying max(YoY − K, 0) or max(K − YoY, 0) on one year's inflation.
Normal (Bachelier) vol
Volatility measured in absolute percentage points per year, used when the underlying can go negative.
Inflation01
PV change for a 1bp rise in forward inflation rates.
