Call / put
The right to buy (call) or sell (put) the underlying at the strike on or before expiry.
Strike
The fixed price at which an option can be exercised.
Premium
The price paid up front for an option.
Intrinsic value
What an option would pay if exercised now, for example max(K − S, 0) for a put.
Time value
Premium minus intrinsic value: the value of the chance that the option ends further in the money.
Implied volatility
The volatility that makes the pricing formula match the market price. Options are quoted in it.
American / European
American options can be exercised on any day up to expiry. European options only at expiry.
Put-call parity
Call − Put = Se−qT − Ke−rT for European options with the same strike and expiry.
