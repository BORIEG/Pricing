STIR future
Short-term interest rate future quoted as 100 minus the rate for a future three-month (or one-month) period.
SOFR / SONIA / SARON / €STR
Overnight risk-free reference rates in USD, GBP, CHF and EUR.
IMM date
Third Wednesday of March, June, September and December; start and end of quarterly futures periods.
Tick value
Money value of a one-basis-point price move for one contract ($25 for 3M SOFR).
Convexity adjustment
Amount by which a futures rate exceeds the equivalent forward rate because of daily margining; about ½σ²T₁T₂.
Compounded in arrears
Rate computed after the period ends by compounding each daily overnight fixing.
Price alignment interest
Interest adjustment on cleared products that returns the interest earned on variation margin.
