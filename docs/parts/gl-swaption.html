Payer / receiver swaption
The right to enter a swap paying (payer) or receiving (receiver) the fixed strike rate.
Expiry × tenor
Option life and underlying swap life, for example 2M × 5Y.
Annuity
Σ τiD(ti) over the fixed leg: the PV of 1 per year on each payment date.
Forward swap rate
The fixed rate that makes a forward-starting swap worth zero today.
Vol cube
Swaption implied vols by expiry, tenor and strike.
Physical / cash settlement
On exercise, either a real swap is booked (physical) or its value is paid in cash.
