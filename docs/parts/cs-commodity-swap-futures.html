Commodity swap & spread futures

E[A] = (Σpublished Pd + Σremaining Fd) / D
PV = (E[A] − K) × Q × n  (OTC: × D(Tpay))
Spread: (E[Aprod] − E[Acrude] − K) × Q × n, same units (6.35 bbl/t)

Greeks: delta = (remaining days / D) × Q × n, melting daily; bucketed by underlying futures month; no gamma or vega.
