Black-76
Black-Scholes written on a futures or forward price, with no carry term.
Premium-style
The option premium is paid in cash up front. PV is discounted to the payment date.
Futures-style
No premium is paid. The option is margined daily like a future, so its PV is not discounted.
Point value
The money value of a 1.00 move in a contract's quoted price.
Mid-curve option
A STIR option that expires a year or more before its underlying future.
32nds and 64ths
US Treasury futures prices are quoted in 32nds (103-24 = 103.75). Their options are quoted in 64ths.
Weekly option
A listed option expiring on a given weekday within the month, rather than at the monthly expiry.
