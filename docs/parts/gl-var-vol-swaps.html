Realised volatility
The annualised size of daily log-returns actually observed: √(252/n × Σ r²).
Variance swap
A swap paying Nvar × (realised variance − strike²).
Volatility swap
A swap paying Nvega × (realised vol − strike).
Vega notional
Money per vol point; for a variance swap, Nvar = Nvega/(2K).
Convexity adjustment
The gap between √(var swap level) and the fair vol swap strike, caused by the curvature of the square root.
Log contract
The 1/K²-weighted strip of out-of-the-money options that replicates variance; the basis of the VIX.
