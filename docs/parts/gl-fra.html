FRA
Forward rate agreement: a single fixed-versus-floating exchange on one future interest period, settled as one net payment.
Fixing
The moment a floating rate (STIBOR, EURIBOR, SOFR) is observed and becomes known for a period.
Forward rate
The rate for a future period implied by today's curve: the market's fair expectation for a linear payoff.
Projection curve
The curve used only to compute forward rates of a particular index (for example 3M STIBOR).
Discounting curve
The curve used to bring future payments back to today; the OIS curve for collateralised trades.
