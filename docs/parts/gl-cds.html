Credit event
The trigger for a CDS payout: failure to pay, bankruptcy, or (depending on the contract) restructuring.
Recovery rate
The fraction of face value still worth something after default, set for CDS by an ISDA auction. Assumed 40% for senior debt.
Hazard rate
The default intensity λ: roughly the probability per year of defaulting, given survival so far.
Survival probability
Q(t) = e−λt, the chance the company has not defaulted by time t.
RPV01
Risky annuity: the PV of receiving 1 per year until maturity or default, whichever comes first.
Upfront
Cash paid at the start of a fixed-coupon CDS to settle the difference between the coupon and the market spread.
CS01
PV change for a 1bp widening of the credit spread.
Jump to default (JTD)
PV change if the reference entity defaults immediately.
