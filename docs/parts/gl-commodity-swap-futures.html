Average-price swap
Swap paying the average of a published commodity price over a period against a fixed price.
Pricing day
A day on which the reference price (e.g. Platts Dubai) is published and counted in the average.
1st line
The front-month futures contract on each pricing day; the average rolls to the next contract when the front expires.
Crack spread
Price of a refined product minus the price of crude oil; a refiner's gross margin.
bbl / mt conversion
Fuel oil is quoted per metric tonne; divide by about 6.35 barrels per tonne to compare with crude in $/bbl.
