Straddle
A call plus a put at the same strike; a bet on the size of moves rather than their direction.
Forward volatility
The volatility implied for a future period: √[(σ2²T2 − σ1²T1)/(T2 − T1)].
FVA
Forward volatility agreement: an agreement to enter an at-the-money straddle at a future date at a vol fixed today.
Strike date
The date on which an FVA's straddle strike is set at the then-forward.
Forward smile
The shape of the vol smile expected at a future date; it makes forward-starting options model-dependent.
