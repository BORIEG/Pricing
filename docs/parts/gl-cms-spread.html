CMS rate
Constant maturity swap rate: the market swap rate of a fixed tenor observed on one date, used to set one payment.
Convexity adjustment
The small amount by which a forward CMS rate exceeds the forward swap rate, because the rate is paid once instead of as an annuity.
Curve steepness (30s5s)
The 30-year swap rate minus the 5-year swap rate.
Straddle
A call plus a put at the same strike. It pays on a move in either direction.
Correlation
A number between −1 and +1 measuring how much two rates move together.
