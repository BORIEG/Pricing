Payer / receiver
Options to buy / sell protection at a strike spread; calls / puts on the spread.
Forward risky annuity
The RPV01 of a CDS starting at expiry, weighted by survival from today.
Front-end protection
Losses on defaults between trade and expiry, collected by a payer holder on exercise.
Loss-adjusted forward
The forward spread plus front-end protection divided by the forward annuity; the forward used in Black's formula.
Delta exchange
Trading an option together with its index hedge at an agreed reference level.
