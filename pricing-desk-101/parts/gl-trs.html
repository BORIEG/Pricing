Total return swap (TRS)
Swap where one side receives an asset's income and price change and pays a floating funding rate plus spread.
Equity-linked swap (ELS)
A total return swap on a share, index or custom basket.
Reset price
The asset price recorded at the start of each TRS period, from which performance and funding are measured.
Forward price (bond)
Today's price financed at the repo rate to a future date, minus coupons received on the way.
Net total return (NTR) index
An index whose level includes dividends reinvested after withholding tax.
Independent amount
Initial margin posted by the TRS receiver to cover a sudden move in the asset.
