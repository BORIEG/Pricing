Correlation
A number from −1 to +1 measuring how closely two series of returns move together.
Correlation swap
A swap paying N × (realised correlation − strike), in correlation points.
Implied correlation
Correlation backed out of option vols, for example from the vols of two assets and of their combination.
Quanto
A product on an asset paid in a different currency at a fixed rate; its value depends on the asset/FX correlation.
Corr01
PV change for a 1-point rise in implied correlation.
