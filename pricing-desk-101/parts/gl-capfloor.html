Cap / floor
A strip of options paying max(L − K, 0) (cap) or max(K − L, 0) (floor) on each floating-rate fixing.
Caplet / floorlet
One single-period option within a cap or floor.
Normal (Bachelier) vol
Volatility of a rate measured in basis points per year rather than in percent of the rate.
Flat vol
A single vol quoted for a whole cap. Desks strip it into one vol per caplet expiry.
Shifted lognormal
Black's model applied to rate + shift, so it still works when rates are near or below zero.
