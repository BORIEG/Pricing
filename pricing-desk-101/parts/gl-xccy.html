Notional exchange
The swap of principal amounts at the start and end of a cross-currency swap.
Cross-currency basis
The spread between a currency's FX-implied (USD-collateral) interest rate and its onshore OIS rate; quoted as a spread on the non-USD leg of float-for-float swaps.
MTM (resetting) swap
A cross-currency swap whose USD notional resets to the FX rate each period, with the change paid immediately.
Par floater
A floating leg plus its notional, discounted on the curve that projects its rate, is worth exactly par.
Basis01
PV change for a 1bp move in the cross-currency basis curve.
