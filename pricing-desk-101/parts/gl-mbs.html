Pass-through
A security that passes homeowners' monthly mortgage payments to investors, net of servicing and guarantee fees.
WAC
Weighted-average coupon: the average mortgage rate paid by the borrowers in a pool.
CPR / SMM
Annual and monthly prepayment rates; SMM = 1 − (1 − CPR)1/12.
PSA
A standard prepayment ramp: 100% PSA rises from 0.2% CPR to 6% CPR over 30 months.
TBA
To-be-announced: a forward trade on agency pools where the exact pools are named two days before settlement.
Dollar roll
Selling a TBA for one month and buying it back for the next. The price difference (the drop) acts as a financing rate.
OAS
Option-adjusted spread: the spread over simulated rate paths that reprices an MBS, accounting for prepayment.
Negative convexity
Price rises less when rates fall than it drops when rates rise, because prepayments speed up.
