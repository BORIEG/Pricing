CMS spread options

CMSfwd = fwd swap rate + convexity adj; X = CMS30 − CMS5
σX = √(σ302 + σ52 − 2ρσ30σ5),  d = (X − K)/σX√T
Cap = NτD[(X−K)N(d) + σX√T n(d)]
Floor = NτD[(K−X)N(−d) + σX√T n(d)]; straddle = cap + floor

Greeks: spread delta (+ to the long rate, − to the short rate), vega to each rate's vol, correlation sensitivity (long options are short correlation), theta.
