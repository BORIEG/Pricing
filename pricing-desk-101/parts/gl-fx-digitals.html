Digital (binary) option
Pays a fixed amount if a condition holds at expiry, nothing otherwise.
One-touch
Pays a fixed amount if the rate trades at or through a barrier at any time before expiry.
Reflection principle
The symmetry argument that counts paths which cross a barrier and come back; it gives the one-touch formula.
Dual digital
Pays a fixed amount only if conditions on two different rates are both true at expiry.
Bivariate normal N2
The probability that two correlated bell-curve variables are both below given levels.
Monte Carlo
Pricing by simulating many random paths and averaging the discounted payoffs.
Pin risk
The hedging difficulty when spot sits at a digital's strike close to expiry.
