Options & Black-Scholes

F = Se(r−q)T,  d1,2 = [ln(F/K) ± ½σ2T] / σ√T
Call = Se−qTN(d1) − Ke−rTN(d2)
Put = Ke−rTN(−d2) − Se−qTN(−d1)
Parity: C − P = Se−qT − Ke−rT

Greeks: delta e−qTN(d1) (call), gamma, vega (per vol point), theta (per day), rho. American single-stock options: binomial tree. VIX options: Black-76 on the VIX future of the same expiry. Multiply by the contract size (100 shares, $100 × SPX/VIX, KRW 250,000 × KOSPI 200).
