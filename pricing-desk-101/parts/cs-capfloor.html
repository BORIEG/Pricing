Caps & floors

Fi = (P(si)/P(ei) − 1)/τi,  di = (Fi − K)/σN√Ti
Caplet = NτiD(ei)[(Fi−K)N(di) + σN√Ti n(di)]
Floorlet = NτiD(ei)[(K−Fi)N(−di) + σN√Ti n(di)]
Cap/floor = Σ caplets/floorlets; cap − floor = payer swap at K

Greeks: vega per bp normal vol bucketed by caplet expiry (main risk), delta as a strip of FRA DV01s weighted by N(di), positive gamma and negative theta for the buyer.
