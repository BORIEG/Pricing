Garman-Kohlhagen
Black-Scholes for FX: the foreign interest rate plays the role of the dividend yield.
Foreign / domestic currency
In USD:INR, USD is foreign (base) and INR domestic (quote): the price of 1 USD in INR.
Pips
An FX option price in domestic currency per unit of foreign notional.
Risk reversal (25RR)
Implied vol of the 25-delta call minus that of the 25-delta put.
Butterfly (25BF)
Average extra vol of the 25-delta wings over ATM: a measure of fat tails.
Premium-adjusted delta
Spot delta minus the premium expressed in foreign currency, used when the premium is paid in the foreign currency.
Cut
The time of day an FX option expires, such as New York 10am or Tokyo 3pm.
