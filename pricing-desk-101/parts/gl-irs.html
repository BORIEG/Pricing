Interest rate swap
Exchange of fixed-rate for floating-rate interest payments on the same notional over several years.
OIS
Overnight index swap: the floating leg compounds a daily overnight rate (SOFR, SONIA, €STR) over each period.
Payer / receiver
The side that pays / receives the fixed rate.
Annuity (PV01)
Sum of accrual fractions times discount factors over the fixed leg; PV of receiving 1 per year.
Par swap rate
Fixed rate that makes a new swap worth zero; the rate quoted on screens.
Bucketed delta
DV01 broken down by curve tenor, used to hedge each part of the curve.
Swap spread
Swap rate minus the government bond yield of the same maturity.
Lookback
Using overnight fixings from a few days earlier so the floating amount is known before the payment date.
