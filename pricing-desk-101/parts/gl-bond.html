Coupon
The fixed interest a bond pays each period, quoted per 100 face.
Accrued interest
Coupon earned since the last payment, paid by the buyer to the seller.
Clean / dirty price
Dirty is what you pay; clean is dirty minus accrued interest and is what screens show.
Yield to maturity
The single discount rate that makes a bond's cashflows equal its dirty price.
Modified duration
Percentage price change for a 1% change in yield.
Convexity
How much the price-yield relationship bends; positive convexity helps the holder.
Z-spread
Constant spread over the zero curve that reprices a bond to its market price.
