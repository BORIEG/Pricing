A = Σ τiD(ti),  S = (D(tstart) − D(tend))/A (OIS)
d = (S − K)/σN√T
Payer = NA[(S−K)N(d) + σN√T n(d)]
Receiver = NA[(K−S)N(−d) + σN√T n(d)]
Payer − receiver = forward swap; NDSwaption: PVUSD = PVlocal/FX

Greeks: delta in DV01 (NAN(d) × 1bp, hedged with forward swaps), vega per bp normal vol bucketed on the expiry × tenor grid, positive gamma and negative theta for the buyer.
