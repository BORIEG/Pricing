FX digitals, touches & dual digitals

Digital call = P·Dd·N(d2), put = P·Dd·N(−d2)
One-touch (up, paid at expiry) = P·Dd·[N((−b+νT)/σ√T) + e2νb/σ²·N((−b−νT)/σ√T)], b = ln(H/S), ν = rd − rf − ½σ²
Dual digital = P·D·N2(sAd2,A, sBd2,B, sAsBρ)

Greeks: spiky delta and gamma near the strike or barrier close to expiry, vega that changes sign with moneyness, corr01 for dual digitals. A touch costs about 2 × the digital when drift is small.
