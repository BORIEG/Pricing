Part 6 · Vanilla options

Swaptions

An option to enter an interest rate swap at a fixed rate agreed today. It is one option on one number, the forward swap rate, scaled by the swap's annuity.

Level 1 The idea

A pension fund knows it will receive new cash in two months. It wants to invest it at a fixed rate for 5 years, and it fears rates will fall before then. It buys a receiver swaption: the right, on 26 Jan 2027, to enter a 5-year swap where it receives 4.5% fixed and pays SOFR.

If in January the market 5-year swap rate is below 4.5%, exercising gives it a swap that pays more than the market. If rates are higher, it walks away and invests at the better market rate.

  • Payer swaption: the right to pay fixed K. Worth more when rates rise. It is a call on the swap rate.
  • Receiver swaption: the right to receive fixed K. Worth more when rates fall. It is a put on the swap rate.

A mortgage rate lock with an exit. The bank promises you can fix your mortgage at 4.5% when you sign in two months' time, but you are free to take a better rate if one appears. You pay a small fee today for that promise.

"2M into 5Y" or "2M×5Y" means a swaption expiring in 2 months on a 5-year swap. The expiry is the option's life. The tenor is the life of the swap you get.

Level 2 The cashflows

Today the buyer pays a premium. At expiry, if exercised, a whole swap appears: five annual fixed payments of 4.5% and five annual SOFR payments. Every one of those flows is contingent on the exercise decision.

Level 3 The PV formula

Step 1 · The annuity: the value of 1bp a year

The annuity A is the PV of receiving 1 (per unit of notional) on every fixed payment date, weighted by each period's year fraction:

A = Σi τi × D(ti)

Multiply it by notional × 0.0001 and you get the DV01 of the swap. For a 5-year swap A ≈ 4.5: 1bp a year for 5 years is worth about 4.5bp today.

Step 2 · The forward swap rate

The forward swap rate S is the fixed rate that makes the forward-starting swap worth zero. For an OIS swap like SOFR, the floating leg is worth D(start) − D(end), so:

S = D(tstart) − D(tend)A

With a separate projection curve (TWD 3M, EURIBOR), replace the numerator by the PV of the projected floating leg (see swaps).

Step 3 · What the swap is worth at expiry

A receiver swap at K is worth fixed leg minus floating leg. The fixed leg is N × A × K. The floating leg is, by definition of S, N × A × S. So at expiry:

Receiver payoff = N × A × max(K − S, 0)     Payer payoff = N × A × max(S − K, 0)

Step 4 · Bachelier on the swap rate, with the annuity as discount factor

The payoff is an option on S multiplied by A. Quants proved that you can price it with Bachelier and simply use A where a discount factor would normally go. This is the "annuity measure", and it is how every rates desk prices swaptions.

European swaption, normal model d = S − KσN√T
Payer = N × A × [ (S − K) N(d) + σN√T n(d) ]
Receiver = N × A × [ (K − S) N(−d) + σN√T n(d) ]
T
time to option expiry in years
σN
normal vol for this expiry, tenor and strike, in bp per year (95bp = 0.0095)
n(d)
height of the bell curve, see the toolkit

Payer − receiver = forward swap. Long a payer and short a receiver at the same strike is a forward-starting payer swap: N × A × (S − K). Use it to check your numbers.

Level 4 The two flavours

Swaption · USD SOFR, physically settled

European 5Y USD P SOFRCMP-1D/R 4.5 % 26/Jan/27 reads: European exercise, 5-year underlying swap, USD. The holder would Pay daily-compounded SOFR and Receive 4.5%, so it is a receiver swaption. It expires on 26 Jan 2027. On exercise, a real SOFR swap is booked and cleared. The worked example below prices this trade with illustrative market data.

NDSwaption · non-deliverable, settled in USD

European 5Y TWD P TDCCON-3M/R 2.815 % 19/Feb/30: a receiver swaption on a 5-year Taiwan dollar swap against a 3-month TWD floating rate (TDCCON-3M), strike 2.815%, expiring 19 Feb 2030. TWD cannot be freely delivered offshore. So the underlying is a non-deliverable swap, and on exercise the option is cash-settled in USD.

The pricing formula is the same, with TWD curves and quarterly ACT/365 fixed periods. Convert the TWD PV into USD at the FX rate. Example: TWD 1bn notional, flat TWD zero 2.0%, so A = 4.4499 and S = 2.005%. Expiry in 3.244 years, normal vol 45bp. Then d = −0.999 and the rate value is 0.8776%, giving PV = TWD 39.05m = USD 1.24m at 31.50 TWD per USD.

Level 5 Desk reality

  • The vol cube. Each swaption has its own vol for its expiry, its tenor and its strike. Desks store an ATM grid (expiry × tenor) and a smile at each point. Pick the vol for your 2M × 5Y at your strike.
  • Quoting. Brokers quote ATM swaptions in bp normal vol, and premiums in bp of notional ("2M5Y receivers at 222bp upfront"). Premium is paid on the spot date.
  • Settlement. USD swaptions settle physically into a cleared swap. EUR and GBP often settle in cash using a formula agreed by the market. Non-deliverable currencies settle in USD cash. The settlement style changes the annuity you should use a little, but desks treat it as a small adjustment.
  • Expiry time. 11am New York for USD, 11am London for EUR. Exercise must be notified before then.
  • Beyond European. Bermudan swaptions (exercisable on several dates) need a full model such as Hull-White. They sit inside callable bonds and cancellable swaps.

Worked example

Valuation date 23 Nov 2026. Long USD 100m receiver swaption, strike 4.5%, expiry 26 Jan 2027 (64 days, T = 0.17534). The swap starts on 28 Jan 2027 with five annual fixed payments, ACT/360. Illustrative flat SOFR zero curve 4.00% (continuous, ACT/365). Normal vol 95bp.

  1. Fixed-leg schedule and discount factors.
    28 Jan 28 τ 1.01389 D 0.953865 28 Jan 29 τ 1.01667 D 0.916363 28 Jan 30 τ 1.01389 D 0.880432 28 Jan 31 τ 1.01389 D 0.845910 28 Jan 32 τ 1.01389 D 0.812741
  2. Annuity.
    A = Σ τ × D = 4.473098
  3. Forward swap rate.
    S = (D(28 Jan 27) − D(28 Jan 32)) / A = (0.992793 − 0.812741) / 4.473098 = 4.0252%
  4. Bachelier inputs.
    σN√T = 0.0095 × √0.17534 = 0.003978 d = (0.040252 − 0.045) / 0.003978 = −1.19351 N(−d) = 0.883665 n(d) = 0.195700
  5. Receiver rate value.
    (0.045 − 0.040252) × 0.883665 + 0.003978 × 0.195700 = 0.0041955 + 0.0007785 = 0.0049740
  6. Scale by notional and annuity.
    PV = 100,000,000 × 4.473098 × 0.0049740 = USD 2,224,909 intrinsic = 100,000,000 × 4.473098 × 0.0047478 = USD 2,123,744 → time value USD 101,165

The receiver swaption is worth USD 2,224,909, or 222.5bp of notional. Most of it is intrinsic value, because the market rate (4.03%) is already 47bp below the strike.

Remember

  • A swaption is an option on the forward swap rate. Payer = call on the rate, receiver = put.
  • Annuity A = Σ τiD(ti). Forward swap rate S = PV(floating leg) ÷ A.
  • PV = N × A × Bachelier(S, K, T, σN). Payer − receiver = forward swap.
  • Vol comes from the cube: expiry × tenor × strike. Delta is quoted in DV01, vega per bp of normal vol.
  • NDSwaptions price the same way in local currency, then convert to USD cash settlement.
