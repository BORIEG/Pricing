Part 2 · Futures

Short-rate & swap futures

Exchange-traded bets on where overnight interest rates will average over a future three-month window. They are among the most heavily traded contracts in the world. The ERIS swap future at the end of the page applies the same idea to a whole swap.

Level 1 The idea

Banks lend each other money overnight. The average rate they charge is published every morning: SOFR in dollars, SONIA in sterling, €STR in euros, SARON in Swiss francs. A STIR future (short-term interest rate future) lets you bet on what that rate will average over a future quarter, for example June to September 2027.

The odd part is the quote. The price is 100 minus the rate. If the market expects 3.50%, the future trades at 96.50. This keeps the usual rule that buyers win when the price goes up. Buying the future means you win when rates fall.

It is like betting on the average temperature for next summer, scored as "100 minus degrees". A cool summer gives a high score. If you expect a cool summer, you buy the score.

Level 2 The cashflows

One CME 3M SOFR contract behaves like a deposit of $1,000,000 for a quarter (90/360 of a year). One basis point (0.01%) on that is $1,000,000 × 0.0001 × 0.25 = $25. So every 0.01 move in price pays or costs $25 per contract, settled daily as variation margin, exactly like the futures basics.

At the end of the reference quarter the exchange computes the final rate R from the daily fixings and settles at 100 − R.

Level 3 The PV formula

Step 1 · Position value

Like every future: price change × value of a point × lots PV = (Pnow − Ptrade) × 100 × TV × n
P
futures price, e.g. 96.4812
TV
tick value per basis point: $25 (3M SOFR), €25 (3M Euribor), CHF 25 (3M SARON ICE), £12.50 (3M SONIA ICE)
100
converts a price move of 1.00 into 100 basis points

Step 2 · Fair rate: the forward compounded overnight rate

The final rate compounds every daily fixing over the quarter. If you invest $1 overnight every day from T1 to T2, your expected growth is exactly what the OIS curve says. So the expected rate is the OIS forward:

Forward rate from OIS discount factors F = 1τ ( D(T1)D(T2) − 1 ),   τ = days/360

Step 3 · Add the convexity adjustment

Daily margining creates a small bias. When rates rise, the short side of the future gains margin cash just when cash earns more interest. When rates fall, the short side loses cash just when it earns less. That always helps the short, so the market charges for it. The futures rate sits slightly above the forward rate.

Futures price, desk formula (simple Ho-Lee approximation) P = 100 − 100 × ( F + 12 σN2 T1 T2 )
σN
normal (basis-point) volatility of rates, e.g. 0.0090 = 90bp a year
T1, T2
start and end of the reference quarter in years (ACT/365)

For a contract six months away it is a fraction of a basis point. Five years out it reaches about 10bp, which matters a lot.

Level 4 Desk reality

  • IMM dates. Quarterly contracts (Mar, Jun, Sep, Dec) start their reference period on the third Wednesday of the month. Month codes are H, M, U, Z, so SOFR Jun27 is SFRM7.
  • In-arrears contracts. 3M SOFR, SONIA and SARON futures fix after the reference quarter, from daily compounded fixings. 3M Euribor futures are older: they fix on one day, at the start, on a term rate that includes bank credit risk.
  • Partly fixed contracts. Once inside its quarter, part of R is known. Compound the published fixings, then the forward for the remaining days: R = (1/τ)[ Πpast(1 + riδi) × D(today)/D(T2) − 1 ]. The delta shrinks day by day.
  • 1M SOFR futures average (not compound) SOFR over a calendar month. Each bp is worth $41.67 per contract.
  • Curve building. STIR futures are a main input of the OIS curve between 3 months and 3 years. Convexity-adjusted futures rates are stripped into discount factors.
  • Packs and bundles. Strips of four consecutive quarters (packs) hedge a 1-year swap. Bundles hedge multi-year swaps.

Worked example

On 23 Nov 2026 you are long 20 SOFR Jun27 futures bought at 96.40. The reference quarter is 16 Jun 2027 (205 days away) to 15 Sep 2027 (296 days, so 91 days long). The USD OIS curve is flat at a 3.55% continuously compounded zero rate. SOFR normal volatility is 90bp.

  1. Times and accrual.
    T1 = 205/365 = 0.56164 T2 = 296/365 = 0.81096 τ = 91/360 = 0.25278
  2. Forward compounded SOFR.
    F = (e^(−0.0355×0.56164) / e^(−0.0355×0.81096) − 1) / 0.25278 = 3.51691%
  3. Convexity adjustment.
    CA = ½ × 0.0090² × 0.56164 × 0.81096 = 0.0000184 = 0.18bp
  4. Fair futures price.
    P = 100 − 100 × (0.0351691 + 0.0000184) = 100 − 3.51876 = 96.4812
  5. Position value.
    PV = (96.4812 − 96.40) × 100 × $25 × 20 = 8.12bp × $500 = $4,062

PV +$4,062. The position loses $500 per bp if rates rise.

Level 5 ERIS swap futures (InterestSwapFuture)

A STIR future covers one quarter. An ERIS SOFR swap future wraps a whole standard SOFR swap (for example 7 years, $100,000 notional) into a futures contract. Buying it is economically like receiving fixed on that swap: you gain when rates fall.

Its price is built from the swap's value, so you price it with the interest rate swap page:

ERIS settlement price (per 100 of notional) P = 100 + A + B − C
A
NPV of the underlying receive-fixed swap (fixed coupon set by the exchange), per 100 notional
B
fixed and floating amounts already exchanged since the swap's effective date, accumulated
C
price alignment interest: a running correction that gives back the interest on margin, so the contract behaves like a cleared OTC swap

Position value = (Pnow − Ptrade)/100 × $100,000 × lots. DV01 per lot ≈ $100,000 × annuity × 0.0001, about $60 for a 7-year swap. The example trade ERIS SOFR 7yr Sep24 is a contract listed in September 2024 on a 7-year swap.

Remember

  • Price = 100 − rate. Buyers profit when rates fall.
  • Fair rate = OIS forward over the IMM quarter + convexity adjustment ½σ²T₁T₂.
  • PV = price change × 100 × tick value × lots, with $25 per bp for 3M SOFR.
  • Risk sits in one forward quarter. A pack of four hedges a 1-year swap.
  • ERIS swap futures are a cleared swap in futures form. Price them as a receiver swap.
