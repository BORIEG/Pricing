Part 3 · Swaps

Total return & equity-linked swaps

Get everything an asset pays, its income and its price change, without buying it. In exchange you pay interest as if you had borrowed the money to buy it. Desks book bond versions as TRS and equity, index and basket versions as ELS.

Level 1 The idea

Suppose you think a US Treasury bond will rally. You could borrow USD 10m and buy it. Or a bank could buy it and promise to pass you everything it does: every coupon, and any rise or fall in its price. In return you pay the bank interest on the money it spent, at SOFR plus a small spread.

Economically you own the bond on borrowed money. You never hold it, you put up little cash, and the bank keeps it on its balance sheet. That is a total return swap. You are the total return receiver, and the bank is the total return payer.

An equity-linked swap (ELS) is the same deal on a share, an index such as the CSI 500 (Chinese mid-caps), or a custom basket designed by a bank.

It's like renting a car on a deal where, when you hand it back, you get any rise in its resale value and pay any fall. You pay the owner a monthly fee equal to interest on the car's price. You drive it as if it were yours, without paying for it up front.

Level 2 The cashflows

Bond TRS (TRS)

The trade runs in periods, often a month or a quarter. At the start of each period the bond's price is recorded (P0, the reset price). During the period any coupon is passed to you. At the end:

  • the total return leg pays you Pend − P0. If the price fell, this is negative and you pay it;
  • the funding leg charges you P0 × (SOFR + s) × days/360.

Then the price resets and the next period starts from the new price.

Equity-linked swap (ELS)

Same structure. The performance leg pays N × (Iend ÷ I0 − 1), where I is the share, index or basket level. Dividends are added if the index is a price index. They are already inside the level for a net total return (NTR) index such as CSI 500 NTR. You pay SOFR + spread on N.

Level 3 The PV formula

We price one period. A multi-period trade is just the current period, because each later period restarts from the market price and is worth roughly zero apart from its spread.

Bond TRS

Step 1 · the expected price at the end is the bond's forward price Pfwd = Pnow − c × Drepo(tc)Drepo(T)

This is what it costs to buy the bond today, financed in the repo market (see repo), minus the coupon you'd collect on the way. The repo rate is the bond's own financing rate.

Step 2 · total return leg, discounted on SOFR OIS PVTR = (Pfwd − P0) × D(T) + c × D(tc)
Step 3 · funding leg: compounded SOFR + spread on P₀ PVfund = P0 × [ (1 − D(T)) + s τ D(T) ]
Step 4 · PV for the total return receiver PV = N100 × ( PVTR − PVfund )

At the reset (Pnow = P0) the algebra collapses to PV ≈ N/100 × P0 × τ × D × (repo − SOFR − s). So the fair TRS spread ≈ the bond's repo rate − SOFR. The receiver pays what it would cost the bank to finance the bond, plus any balance-sheet charge.

Equity-linked swap

Forward of the underlying F = Inow × e(r − q)T    or, for a futures underlier,   F = futures price
PV for the performance receiver PV = N × [ D(T) (FI0 − 1) − (1 − D(T)) − s τ D(T) ] = N × [ InowI0 e−qT − 1 − s τ D(T) ]
q
carry of the underlying: the dividend yield for a price index, about 0 for a total-return index, or the yield implied by futures when futures trade away from fair value (common for CSI 500)
s
spread over SOFR. Its fair value is (e−qT − 1) ÷ (τD). Anything above that is the bank's hedging and balance-sheet charge.

Level 4 Desk reality

  • Reading the trade description. TRS T 4.625 02/15/35 is a TRS on the US Treasury 4.625% February 2035. ELS underliers seen live include dealer custom baskets (for example a US wage-growth basket), the CSI 500 NTR index, and even a futures contract (SHFE Deformed Bar Fut, steel rebar).
  • Live periods. Midway through a period, the SOFR already accrued since the reset is known. Add it as a fixed amount, and use only the remaining days in the formulas.
  • Clean vs dirty. Bond TRS contracts specify whether performance is measured on clean or dirty price. With dirty prices the coupon drops out of the price on payment day and is paid separately. Count it once.
  • Dividends and tax. On price-return equity swaps the receiver typically gets a percentage of gross dividends (net of withholding tax). An NTR index has net dividends already reinvested.
  • Funding curve. SOFR (or €STR or SONIA) compounded, ACT/360, paid at each reset. Spreads reflect the bank's balance-sheet cost and, for markets like China A-shares, quota and access costs.
  • Collateral. These trades usually carry an independent amount (initial margin) that the receiver posts, sized to a few days of the asset's volatility.

Worked example

Bond TRS receiver

Today is the reset. You receive the total return on USD 10m face of T 4.625% Feb-35 for 92 days, paying SOFR + 15bp. Reset dirty price P0 = Pnow = 101.20. A coupon of 2.3125 is paid in 84 days. SOFR 3.70%, the bond's repo rate 3.80%, both flat and continuously compounded (curve time ACT/365, funding accrual ACT/360).

  1. Forward price: finance the bond at repo, minus the coupon.
    c × D_repo(84d) = 2.3125 × e^(−0.038×84/365) = 2.29236 P_fwd = (101.20 − 2.29236) × e^(0.038×92/365) = 99.8595
  2. Total return leg, discounted on SOFR.
    D(92d) = e^(−0.037×92/365) = 0.990717 PV_TR = (99.8595 − 101.20) × 0.990717 + 2.3125 × e^(−0.037×84/365) = 0.96487
  3. Funding leg: SOFR part plus spread part.
    101.20 × (1 − 0.990717) = 0.93941 101.20 × 0.0015 × 92/360 × 0.990717 = 0.03843 PV_fund = 0.97784
  4. PV for 10m face.
    PV = 100,000 × (0.96487 − 0.97784) = −USD 1,297
  5. Fair spread: the s that makes PV zero.
    s_fair = (0.96487 − 0.93941) / (101.20 × 92/360 × 0.990717) = 9.94bp (≈ repo − SOFR = 10bp)

The receiver's TRS is worth −USD 1,297: paying 15bp against a fair 9.94bp costs 5.06bp × USD 256 per bp.

ELS on CSI 500 NTR

  1. USD 5m notional, 1 year, receive index performance, pay SOFR + 60bp. NTR index, so q = 0. SOFR 3.70%.
    D(1y) = e^(−0.037) = 0.963676 τ = 365/360 = 1.013889
  2. PV at the reset (Inow = I0).
    PV = 5,000,000 × (1 − 1 − 0.006 × 1.013889 × 0.963676) = −USD 29,312

The receiver starts −USD 29,312 down: the 60bp charge over the zero fair spread. Delta is USD 50,000 per 1% move in the index.

Remember

  • TRS receiver = owning the asset on borrowed money: gets income and price change, pays SOFR + spread.
  • PV = expected performance (from the forward price) + income − funding, all discounted on OIS.
  • Fair bond TRS spread ≈ repo − SOFR. Fair ELS spread depends on the carry q.
  • Delta is the full asset delta. The cash outlay is only the margin.
