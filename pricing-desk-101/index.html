Pricing Desk 101

Every major product type a multi-asset trading desk deals in, explained from scratch. It runs from a share of stock to a dual-digital FX barrier. By the last page you should be able to write down the cashflows of any of them and compute its present value (PV) by hand.

How every product page works

Each page climbs the same four levels. You can stop at any level and still have a correct picture. Each new level only adds detail.

StepWhat you get
Level 1 The ideaWhat the product is for, in everyday words, with an analogy.
Level 2 The cashflowsEvery payment drawn on a timeline: who pays, who receives, when, and how much.
Level 3 The PV formulaThe formula built step by step: estimate each unknown payment, then discount it to today.
Level 4 Desk realityThe conventions that change the number on a real trade: day counts, curves, lags, quoting.
ThenThe greeks that matter for that product, a worked example done by hand, a live calculator, and a short recap.

How to read the cashflow diagrams

Every diagram is drawn from your side of the trade. Time runs left to right. Arrow length is proportional to the amount.

Every PV in this course is the same recipe. List the payments, replace each unknown amount by its market expectation (a forward, a survival-weighted amount, or an option formula), multiply each by its discount factor, and add them up.
