Reference

Cheat sheet & glossary

Every PV formula and the greeks that matter, one card per page, in course order. Below them, every term the course defines.

Formulas

Notation used throughout: N notional, τ accrual year fraction, D(t) discount factor from the OIS curve, F forward, K strike or fixed rate, T time to expiry in years, N(x) the normal probability table.

The pricing toolkit

PV = Σ CFi × D(ti)
F = (P(T1)/P(T2) − 1) / τ
Black call = D[FN(d1) − KN(d2)], d1,2 = [ln(F/K) ± ½σ²T]/σ√T
Bachelier call = D[(F−K)N(d) + σN√T n(d)], d = (F−K)/σN√T

Greeks: DV01 = PV(curve+1bp) − PV; delta, gamma, vega, theta by bump-and-reprice. Parity: C − P = D(F − K).

Shares, preferreds & warrants

PVUSD = q × S ÷ Xlocal per USD
F(T) = Se(r−yd)T
Preferred ≈ min(d/y, call price)
Warrant = N/(N+M) × BS call

Greeks: delta = 1% of value per 1% move; FX delta for foreign listings; preferred DV01 ≈ PV/y × 1bp; warrants carry option delta, vega, theta.

FX spot, forwards & NDFs

F = S × (1 + rquoted/Bq) / (1 + rbased/Bb)
PVquote = N(F − K)Dquote(T)
NDF: PVUSD = N(F − K)/F × DUSD(T)

Greeks: FX delta ≈ N × Dbase; DV01 in each currency (points risk); no gamma or vega.

Bonds & bills

Pdirty = Σ (c/f)/(1+y/f)k+w + 100/(1+y/f)n−1+w
AI = (c/f) × days since coupon / days in period; clean = dirty − AI
Bill: P = 100(1 − d × days/360)

Greeks: DV01 = P(y−1bp) − P(y); Dmod = DV01/(P × 1bp); convexity; CS01 via Z-spread.

Repo & reverse repo

C = face/100 × Pdirty × (1 − h)
R = C(1 + rrepo × d/360)
PV (lender, after start) = R × D(T2); MTM = PV − C

Greeks: small DV01 to short rates; carry = C × r/360 per day; collateral cover; bond delta stays with the borrower.

Futures: the basics

PV = (Fnow − Ftrade) × M × n  (no discounting, settled daily)
F = S e(r − q + u − y)T  · FX: F = S e(rd−rf)T  · stock: (S − PV(divs)) erT

Greeks: delta = M × n per point; small rho and dividend/carry sensitivity; no gamma or vega.

Commodity swap & spread futures

E[A] = (Σpublished Pd + Σremaining Fd) / D
PV = (E[A] − K) × Q × n  (OTC: × D(Tpay))
Spread: (E[Aprod] − E[Acrude] − K) × Q × n, same units (6.35 bbl/t)

Greeks: delta = (remaining days / D) × Q × n, melting daily; bucketed by underlying futures month; no gamma or vega.

Short-rate & swap futures

F = (D(T1)/D(T2) − 1)/τ
P = 100 − 100(F + ½σN2T1T2)
PV = (Pnow − Ptrade) × 100 × TV × n  ($25/bp 3M SOFR)
ERIS: P = 100 + A + B − C

Greeks: DV01 = −TV per long lot, in one forward bucket; small vega through the convexity adjustment.

Bond futures

Bfwd = (P+AI0)(1+rd/360) − c(1+rdc/360)
Ffair = (Bfwd − AIdel)/CF
Invoice = F×CF + AIdel · Net basis = P − F×CF − carry
PV = (Fnow − Ftrade)/100 × face × n

Greeks: DV01 ≈ CTD DV01 / CF per contract; hedge ratio = bond DV01 / futures DV01; small repo sensitivity; negative convexity near a CTD switch.

Forward rate agreements

F = (Pproj(T1)/Pproj(T2) − 1)/τ
PVbuyer = N τ (F − K) D(T2)
Settlement at T1: N(L − K)τ/(1 + Lτ)

Greeks: DV01 ≈ N τ D(T2) × 1bp, all in one forward bucket; gamma ≈ 0.

Interest rate swaps & OIS

A = Σ τi D(Ti)  · Fi = (Di−1/Di − 1)/τi
OIS: S = (D(T0) − D(Tn))/A  · IBOR: S = ΣτjFjprojDj / A
PVpayer = N(S − K)A

Greeks: DV01 ≈ N × A × 1bp (payer gains when rates rise); bucketed delta at maturity pillar; basis delta for IBOR legs; small positive gamma; carry and roll-down.

Non-deliverable swaps

PVUSD = Σ N(K − Fi)τi ÷ Xfwd(ti) × DUSD(ti)
= (1/S) Σ N(K − Fi)τi Dlocal,NDF(ti)

Greeks: local-currency DV01 ≈ (N/S) ΣτD × 1bp; FX delta only on the PV itself; small quanto term.

Cross-currency swaps

PVleg = N[−D(t0) + Σ ciτiD(ti) + D(tn)] per currency
PVUSD = PVUSD leg + PVforeign leg ÷ S
Dforeign(t) = DUSD(t) × S ÷ Xfwd(t)
MTM: PV ≈ Nf(smkt − s)Af ÷ S

Greeks: FX delta (large once notionals exchanged), basis01 ≈ N A × 1bp, DV01 on fixed legs.

Asset swaps

sASW = (Pswap − Pdirty) ÷ (100 × A)
Pswap = Σ c τjD(tj) + 100D(T),  A = Σ τiD(ti)
Position PV = N(straded − sASW)A

Greeks: spread01 ≈ −N A × 1bp; DV01 near zero; jump-to-default ≈ −N(1 − R) ± swap MTM.

Total return & equity-linked swaps

Bond: Pfwd = (Pnow − c Drepo(tc)) ÷ Drepo(T)
PV = N/100 × [(Pfwd − P0)D + c D(tc) − P0((1 − D) + sτD)]
ELS: PV = N[(Inow/I0)e−qT − 1 − sτD]

Greeks: full asset delta (N/100 per point; N × 1% per 1%), bond DV01, spread01 ≈ N P0/100 × τD × 1bp. Fair bond TRS spread ≈ repo − SOFR.

Zero-coupon inflation swaps

Fixed: N[(1+K)T − 1]   Inflation: N[I(T)/Ibase − 1]
Ifwd(T) = Iref(1+b)Trem
PVrec fixed = N D(Trem)[(1+K)T − Ifwd/Ibase]; new trade: fair K = b

Greeks: IE01 ≈ −N D(Ifwd/Ibase)Trem/(1+b) × 1bp; small DV01; fixing risk near maturity.

Credit default swaps

Q(t) = e−λt, λ ≈ s ÷ (1 − R)
RPV01 = Σ τi Di (Qi−1 + Qi)/2
Prot = (1 − R) Σ Di (Qi−1 − Qi)
PVseller = N(c × RPV01 − Prot); upfront = Prot − c × RPV01
Recovery swap: PV = N(Rexp − Rfix) × Prot ÷ (1 − R)

Greeks: CS01 ≈ −N × RPV01 × 1bp (seller); JTD ≈ −N(1 − R) − PV; Rec01; small IR01.

CDS indices

solve flat λ: Prot ÷ RPV01 = Squote
upfront = Prot − c × RPV01; price = 100 × (1 − upfront)
PVseller = f × N(c × RPV01 − Prot), f = names left ÷ n
default: seller pays (1 − R) × N/n

Greeks: CS01 ≈ −fN × RPV01 × 1bp; JTD per name ≈ −(N/n)(1 − R) − PV/n; index basis vs intrinsic.

Index tranches

TL = min(max(L − A, 0), D − A) ÷ (D − A)
L(M) = (1 − R) N((N−1(p) − √ρM) ÷ √(1 − ρ))
ETL = (E[min(L,D)] − E[min(L,A)]) ÷ (D − A)
RPV01 = Σ τiDi(1 − avg ETL); Prot = Σ Di ΔETLi
PVseller = N(c × RPV01 − Prot)

Greeks: index CS01 (leverage / hedge ratio), Correlation01 (equity long, senior short), JTD, recovery sensitivity.

MBS, TBAs & CMOs

SMM = 1 − (1 − CPR)1/12; M = Bg ÷ (1 − (1 + g)−n), g = WAC/12
CFk = Bc/12 + SP + SMM(B − SP)
PV = Σ CFk ÷ (1 + y/12)k
TBA: PV = Face × (Pmkt − Ptrade)/100 × D(settle)
drop ≈ 100c/12 − Prrepo/12 + SMM(100 − P)

Greeks: effective duration / DV01 (prepayments react), negative convexity, prepay sensitivity, short vega, OAS01.

Options & Black-Scholes

F = Se(r−q)T,  d1,2 = [ln(F/K) ± ½σ2T] / σ√T
Call = Se−qTN(d1) − Ke−rTN(d2)
Put = Ke−rTN(−d2) − Se−qTN(−d1)
Parity: C − P = Se−qT − Ke−rT

Greeks: delta e−qTN(d1) (call), gamma, vega (per vol point), theta (per day), rho. American single-stock options: binomial tree. VIX options: Black-76 on the VIX future of the same expiry. Multiply by the contract size (100 shares, $100 × SPX/VIX, KRW 250,000 × KOSPI 200).

Options on futures

Black-76: Call = D[FN(d1) − KN(d2)], Put = D[KN(−d2) − FN(−d1)]
Bachelier (STIR): Put = D[(K−F)N(−d) + σN√T n(d)], d = (F−K)/σN√T
D = e−rT premium-style, 1 futures-style. PV = qty × price × point value

Greeks: delta in futures contracts, gamma (huge for weeklies), vega per vol point or per bp normal vol, theta. Point values: crude $1,000, E-mini Nasdaq $20, 5Y note $1,000 (strikes in 32nds, premium in 64ths), 3M SONIA £1,250. Put on STIR price = call on rate.

FX vanilla options

F = Se(rd−rf)T
Call = Se−rfTN(d1) − Ke−rdTN(d2)
Put = Ke−rdTN(−d2) − Se−rfTN(−d1)
PVdom = Nfor × price; PVfor = PVdom / S

Greeks: delta in foreign ccy (spot, forward or premium-adjusted = spot delta − PVfor%), gamma per 1% spot, vega per vol point bucketed by expiry, domestic and foreign rho. Smile: σ25C/25P = ATM + BF ± ½RR.

Caps & floors

Fi = (P(si)/P(ei) − 1)/τi,  di = (Fi − K)/σN√Ti
Caplet = NτiD(ei)[(Fi−K)N(di) + σN√Ti n(di)]
Floorlet = NτiD(ei)[(K−Fi)N(−di) + σN√Ti n(di)]
Cap/floor = Σ caplets/floorlets; cap − floor = payer swap at K

Greeks: vega per bp normal vol bucketed by caplet expiry (main risk), delta as a strip of FRA DV01s weighted by N(di), positive gamma and negative theta for the buyer.

Swaptions

A = Σ τiD(ti),  S = (D(tstart) − D(tend))/A (OIS)
d = (S − K)/σN√T
Payer = NA[(S−K)N(d) + σN√T n(d)]
Receiver = NA[(K−S)N(−d) + σN√T n(d)]
Payer − receiver = forward swap; NDSwaption: PVUSD = PVlocal/FX

Greeks: delta in DV01 (NAN(d) × 1bp, hedged with forward swaps), vega per bp normal vol bucketed on the expiry × tenor grid, positive gamma and negative theta for the buyer.

CMS spread options

CMSfwd = fwd swap rate + convexity adj; X = CMS30 − CMS5
σX = √(σ302 + σ52 − 2ρσ30σ5),  d = (X − K)/σX√T
Cap = NτD[(X−K)N(d) + σX√T n(d)]
Floor = NτD[(K−X)N(−d) + σX√T n(d)]; straddle = cap + floor

Greeks: spread delta (+ to the long rate, − to the short rate), vega to each rate's vol, correlation sensitivity (long options are short correlation), theta.

CDS index options

A = Σti > Te τiDi(Qi−1 + Qi)/2; F = fwd Prot ÷ A
FEP = (1 − R)D(Te)(1 − Q(Te)); F′ = F + FEP/A
Payer = A[F′N(d1) − KN(d2)]
Receiver = A[KN(−d2) − F′N(−d1)]
Payer − Receiver = A(F′ − K)

Greeks: delta in CS01 terms (NAN(d1) × 1bp for a payer), gamma, vega = NAF′n(d1)√T/100, theta.

LPI: capped & floored inflation

Xi = min(max(yi, 0%), 5%) = yi − Caplet(5%) + Floorlet(0%)
E[Xi] = fi − Capi + Floori  (Bachelier on the forward YoY rate)
PV = N·D(T)·{[(1+K)T − 1] − [∏(1 + E[Xi]) − 1]}  (LPI payer; years treated as independent)

Greeks: inflation01 (damped by the clipping), normal vega on the YoY options, DV01, year-to-year inflation correlation.

FX digitals, touches & dual digitals

Digital call = P·Dd·N(d2), put = P·Dd·N(−d2)
One-touch (up, paid at expiry) = P·Dd·[N((−b+νT)/σ√T) + e2νb/σ²·N((−b−νT)/σ√T)], b = ln(H/S), ν = rd − rf − ½σ²
Dual digital = P·D·N2(sAd2,A, sBd2,B, sAsBρ)

Greeks: spiky delta and gamma near the strike or barrier close to expiry, vega that changes sign with moneyness, corr01 for dual digitals. A touch costs about 2 × the digital when drift is small.

Forward volatility agreements

σf² = (σ2²T2 − σ1²T1) / (T2 − T1)
Straddle(σ) = 2FD·[2N(½σ√τ) − 1] ≈ 0.8·σ√τ·F·D
PV = N·[Straddle(σf) − Straddle(Kvol)]

Greeks: forward vega (long σ2, short σ1), almost no delta or gamma before the strike date, vol-of-vol convexity.

Variance & volatility swaps

σreal = 100·√(252/n · Σ ln(Si/Si−1)²)
Var: PV = D·Nvar·([neσe² + (n − ne)σimp²]/n − K²), Nvar = Nvega/2K
Vol: PV = D·Nvega·(√Kvar·(1 − ν²/8) − Kvol)

Greeks: vega (≈ D·Nvar·2σimp·remaining fraction, shrinks as days are realised), constant cash gamma, theta = realised vs implied each day, vol of vol for vol swaps.

Correlation swaps

ρreal = Σ(x − x̄)(y − ȳ) / √(Σ(x − x̄)² · Σ(y − ȳ)²)
PV = D·N·(w·ρe + (1 − w)·ρimp − K)
ρimp = (σ1² + σ2² − σ12²) / (2σ1σ2)

Greeks: corr01 = D·N·(1 − w); vega and delta close to zero.

Structured products & other records

Protected note PV = N·π·D(T) + (N·p/S0)·BScall(K = S0)
Fair p = [N(1 − m) − N·π·D(T)] / (N·BScall/S0)
Cash records (SimpleFlow, Expense, PayToHold): PV = ± amount × D(pay date)

Greeks: note: delta and vega from the calls, large DV01 and issuer CS01 from the bond. Cash records: FX only.

Glossary (190 terms)

1st line
The front-month futures contract on each pricing day; the average rolls to the next contract when the front expires. →
32nds and 64ths
US Treasury futures prices are quoted in 32nds (103-24 = 103.75). Their options are quoted in 64ths. →
Accrued interest
Coupon earned since the last payment, paid by the buyer to the seller. →
American / European
American options can be exercised on any day up to expiry. European options only at expiry. →
Annuity
Σ τiD(ti) over the fixed leg: the PV of 1 per year on each payment date. →
Annuity (PV01)
Sum of accrual fractions times discount factors over the fixed leg; PV of receiving 1 per year. →
Asset swap spread
The spread over SOFR an investor earns after swapping a bond's fixed coupons to floating; a measure of the bond's cheapness to swaps. →
Attachment / detachment
The pool-loss levels where a tranche starts and stops absorbing losses. →
Autocallable
A note that repays early with a coupon if the underlying is above a trigger on an observation date. →
Average-price swap
Swap paying the average of a published commodity price over a period against a fixed price. →
Base correlation
The market convention of quoting one correlation per base (0–K%) tranche. →
Base index
The lagged index level at the start of the swap, against which the final index is compared. →
Basis
Futures price minus spot price; converges to zero at expiry. →
Basis point (bp)
One hundredth of a percent: 0.01% = 0.0001. →
Basis01
PV change for a 1bp move in the cross-currency basis curve. →
bbl / mt conversion
Fuel oil is quoted per metric tonne; divide by about 6.35 barrels per tonne to compare with crude in $/bbl. →
Bivariate normal N2
The probability that two correlated bell-curve variables are both below given levels. →
Black-76
Black-Scholes written on a futures or forward price, with no carry term. →
Breakeven inflation
The fixed rate of a new zero-coupon inflation swap; the inflation rate at which nominal and inflation-linked bonds return the same. →
Bucketed delta
DV01 broken down by curve tenor, used to hedge each part of the curve. →
Butterfly (25BF)
Average extra vol of the 25-delta wings over ATM: a measure of fat tails. →
Call / put
The right to buy (call) or sell (put) the underlying at the strike on or before expiry. →
Cap / floor
A strip of options paying max(L − K, 0) (cap) or max(K − L, 0) (floor) on each floating-rate fixing. →
Capital protection
The guaranteed share of the notional repaid at maturity, as long as the issuer does not default. →
Caplet / floorlet
One single-period option within a cap or floor. →
Cheapest-to-deliver (CTD)
The bond in a futures delivery basket that is cheapest for the seller to deliver; the one with the highest implied repo. →
Clean / dirty price
Dirty is what you pay; clean is dirty minus accrued interest and is what screens show. →
CMS rate
Constant maturity swap rate: the market swap rate of a fixed tenor observed on one date, used to set one payment. →
Compounded in arrears
Rate computed after the period ends by compounding each daily overnight fixing. →
Contango / backwardation
Futures curve sloping up (later months dearer) / sloping down (later months cheaper). →
Convenience yield
Implied benefit of holding a physical commodity now rather than later; lowers the futures price. →
Conversion factor
Exchange-set multiplier (price per 1 face at a 6% yield) that scales the invoice price for each deliverable bond. →
Convexity
How much the price-yield relationship bends; positive convexity helps the holder. →
Convexity adjustment
Amount by which a futures rate exceeds the equivalent forward rate because of daily margining; about ½σ²T₁T₂. →
Corr01
PV change for a 1-point rise in implied correlation. →
Correlation
A number between −1 and +1 measuring how much two rates move together. →
Correlation swap
A swap paying N × (realised correlation − strike), in correlation points. →
Cost of carry
Funding cost plus storage minus income from holding the underlying until expiry; links spot to the fair futures price. →
Coupon
The fixed interest a bond pays each period, quoted per 100 face. →
Covered interest parity
The no-arbitrage link between spot, forward and the two currencies' interest rates. →
CPR / SMM
Annual and monthly prepayment rates; SMM = 1 − (1 − CPR)1/12. →
Crack spread
Price of a refined product minus the price of crude oil; a refiner's gross margin. →
Credit event
The trigger for a CDS payout: failure to pay, bankruptcy, or (depending on the contract) restructuring. →
Cross-currency basis
The spread between a currency's FX-implied (USD-collateral) interest rate and its onshore OIS rate; quoted as a spread on the non-USD leg of float-for-float swaps. →
CS01
PV change for a 1bp widening of the credit spread. →
Curve steepness (30s5s)
The 30-year swap rate minus the 5-year swap rate. →
Cut
The time of day an FX option expires, such as New York 10am or Tokyo 3pm. →
Day count
The rule that turns two dates into a year fraction, such as ACT/360 or 30/360. →
Default correlation
How much companies tend to default together. It sets the shape of the pool loss distribution. →
Delivery options
The seller's choices of which bond and which day to deliver, which lower the futures price. →
Delta exchange
Trading an option together with its index hedge at an agreed reference level. →
Digital (binary) option
Pays a fixed amount if a condition holds at expiry, nothing otherwise. →
Dilution
The reduction in each share's slice when new shares are created. →
Discount factor
Today's value of one unit of currency paid at a future date. →
Discounting curve
The curve used to bring future payments back to today; the OIS curve for collateralised trades. →
Dividend
A share of company profits paid to shareholders. →
Dollar roll
Selling a TBA for one month and buying it back for the next. The price difference (the drop) acts as a financing rate. →
Down-and-in put
A put that only comes alive if the underlying falls through a barrier during its life. →
Dual digital
Pays a fixed amount only if conditions on two different rates are both true at expiry. →
Equity, mezzanine, senior
Tranches ordered from first-loss (riskiest) to last-loss (safest). →
Equity-linked swap (ELS)
A total return swap on a share, index or custom basket. →
Ex-date
The first day a share trades without the right to the next dividend. →
Expected tranche loss
The average fraction of a tranche lost by a date, over all default scenarios. →
Expiry × tenor
Option life and underlying swap life, for example 2M × 5Y. →
Fixing
The official reference rate used to settle an NDF or option, such as TAIFX1 or PTAX. →
Flat vol
A single vol quoted for a whole cap. Desks strip it into one vol per caplet expiry. →
Foreign / domestic currency
In USD:INR, USD is foreign (base) and INR domestic (quote): the price of 1 USD in INR. →
Forward points
Forward minus spot, quoted in pips (× 10,000, or × 100 for JPY pairs). →
Forward price (bond)
Today's price financed at the repo rate to a future date, minus coupons received on the way. →
Forward rate
The rate the curve implies today for a period that starts in the future. →
Forward risky annuity
The RPV01 of a CDS starting at expiry, weighted by survival from today. →
Forward smile
The shape of the vol smile expected at a future date; it makes forward-starting options model-dependent. →
Forward swap rate
The fixed rate that makes a forward-starting swap worth zero today. →
Forward volatility
The volatility implied for a future period: √[(σ2²T2 − σ1²T1)/(T2 − T1)]. →
FRA
Forward rate agreement: a single fixed-versus-floating exchange on one future interest period, settled as one net payment. →
Front-end protection
Losses on defaults between trade and expiry, collected by a payer holder on exercise. →
Futures-style
No premium is paid. The option is margined daily like a future, so its PV is not discounted. →
FVA
Forward volatility agreement: an agreement to enter an at-the-money straddle at a future date at a vol fixed today. →
FX fixing
The official exchange rate published on a set day (for example TAIFX1 for USD/TWD) used to convert local amounts into USD. →
FX swap
A spot or near-date exchange plus the opposite forward exchange. →
Garman-Kohlhagen
Black-Scholes for FX: the foreign interest rate plays the role of the dividend yield. →
General collateral (GC)
Any bond from an accepted basket; its repo rate sits close to OIS. →
GMRA
Global Master Repurchase Agreement, the standard legal contract for repo. →
Gross basis
Bond clean price minus futures price × conversion factor. →
Haircut
The discount applied to collateral value when deciding how much cash to lend. →
Hazard rate
The default intensity λ: roughly the probability per year of defaulting, given survival so far. →
IE01
PV change for a 1bp rise in the breakeven inflation curve. →
IMM date
Third Wednesday of March, June, September and December; start and end of quarterly futures periods. →
Implied correlation
Correlation backed out of option vols, for example from the vols of two assets and of their combination. →
Implied repo
Financing rate earned by buying the bond, selling the future and delivering. →
Implied volatility
The volatility that makes the pricing formula match the market price. Options are quoted in it. →
Independent amount
Initial margin posted by the TRS receiver to cover a sudden move in the asset. →
Index basis
The difference between the quoted index level and its intrinsic value. →
Index factor
Fraction of the original notional still outstanding after defaults: names left ÷ original names. →
Index lag
The delay between a swap date and the month of the price index it references (2 months for UK RPI swaps, 3 months for US CPI and euro HICPx). →
Inflation01
PV change for a 1bp rise in forward inflation rates. →
Initial margin
Deposit held by the clearing house as a cushion against losses; collateral, not a cost. →
Interest rate swap
Exchange of fixed-rate for floating-rate interest payments on the same notional over several years. →
Intrinsic value
The value of an index computed as the average of its constituents' single-name CDS. →
Invoice price
Amount the buyer pays at delivery: futures price × conversion factor + accrued interest. →
Jump to default (JTD)
PV change if the reference entity defaults immediately. →
Large homogeneous pool
The one-factor Gaussian model with very many identical names, which gives pool loss in closed form for each state of the economy. →
Log contract
The 1/K²-weighted strip of out-of-the-money options that replicates variance; the basis of the VIX. →
Lookback
Using overnight fixings from a few days earlier so the floating amount is known before the payment date. →
Loss-adjusted forward
The forward spread plus front-end protection divided by the forward annuity; the forward used in Black's formula. →
LPI (Limited Price Indexation)
UK pension indexation where each year's inflation is clipped into a band, typically [0%, 5%], before compounding. →
Mid-curve option
A STIR option that expires a year or more before its underlying future. →
Modified duration
Percentage price change for a 1% change in yield. →
Monte Carlo
Pricing by simulating many random paths and averaging the discounted payoffs. →
MTM (resetting) swap
A cross-currency swap whose USD notional resets to the FX rate each period, with the change paid immediately. →
Multiplier
Money value of a 1.00 move in a futures price for one contract (¥500 per Nikkei point on the CME yen contract). →
NDF
Non-deliverable forward: only the difference between forward and fixing is paid, usually in USD. →
NDF-implied curve
The local-currency discount curve backed out of offshore NDF prices; it can differ from onshore rates. →
Negative convexity
Price rises less when rates fall than it drops when rates rise, because prepayments speed up. →
Net basis
Gross basis minus carry; approximately the value of the seller's delivery options. →
Net total return (NTR) index
An index whose level includes dividends reinvested after withholding tax. →
Non-deliverable swap (NDS)
An interest rate swap in a restricted currency whose net payments are converted at an FX fixing and paid in USD. →
Normal (Bachelier) vol
Volatility of a rate measured in basis points per year rather than in percent of the rate. →
Notional exchange
The swap of principal amounts at the start and end of a cross-currency swap. →
OAS
Option-adjusted spread: the spread over simulated rate paths that reprices an MBS, accounting for prepayment. →
OIS
Overnight index swap: the floating leg compounds a daily overnight rate (SOFR, SONIA, €STR) over each period. →
OIS curve
Curve built from overnight rates (SOFR, €STR, SONIA), used to discount collateralised trades. →
On-the-run
The newest series of an index, where most trading happens. →
One-touch
Pays a fixed amount if the rate trades at or through a barrier at any time before expiry. →
Par floater
A floating leg plus its notional, discounted on the curve that projects its rate, is worth exactly par. →
Par swap rate
Fixed rate that makes a new swap worth zero; the rate quoted on screens. →
Par-par asset swap
Asset swap package bought for 100 regardless of the bond's price; the price difference is paid upfront. →
Participation
The share of the underlying's rise paid out by a note. →
Pass-through
A security that passes homeowners' monthly mortgage payments to investors, net of servicing and guarantee fees. →
Payer / receiver
The side that pays / receives the fixed rate. →
Payer / receiver swaption
The right to enter a swap paying (payer) or receiving (receiver) the fixed strike rate. →
Physical / cash settlement
On exercise, either a real swap is booked (physical) or its value is paid in cash. →
Pin risk
The hedging difficulty when spot sits at a digital's strike close to expiry. →
Pips
An FX option price in domestic currency per unit of foreign notional. →
Point value
The money value of a 1.00 move in a contract's quoted price. →
Preferred share
A share paying a fixed dividend, ranking ahead of ordinary shares, often perpetual and callable. →
Premium
The price paid up front for an option. →
Premium-adjusted delta
Spot delta minus the premium expressed in foreign currency, used when the premium is paid in the foreign currency. →
Premium-style
The option premium is paid in cash up front. PV is discounted to the payment date. →
Present value (PV)
The sum of all future cashflows, each multiplied by its discount factor. →
Price alignment interest
Interest adjustment on cleared products that returns the interest earned on variation margin. →
Pricing day
A day on which the reference price (e.g. Platts Dubai) is published and counted in the average. →
Proceeds asset swap
Asset swap whose notional equals the bond's dirty price, so no upfront is exchanged. →
Projection curve
The curve used only to compute forward rates of a particular index (for example 3M STIBOR). →
PSA
A standard prepayment ramp: 100% PSA rises from 0.2% CPR to 6% CPR over 30 months. →
Put-call parity
Call − Put = Se−qT − Ke−rT for European options with the same strike and expiry. →
Quanto
A product on an asset paid in a different currency at a fixed rate; its value depends on the asset/FX correlation. →
Quanto adjustment
A small correction to expected values when a payoff in one currency is converted at a rate correlated with it. →
Realised volatility
The annualised size of daily log-returns actually observed: √(252/n × Σ r²). →
Recovery rate
The fraction of face value still worth something after default, set for CDS by an ISDA auction. Assumed 40% for senior debt. →
Reflection principle
The symmetry argument that counts paths which cross a barrier and come back; it gives the one-touch formula. →
Repo
Sale of a bond with an agreement to buy it back later: a secured cash loan. →
Reset price
The asset price recorded at the start of each TRS period, from which performance and funding are measured. →
Reverse repo
The other side of a repo: lending cash and holding the bond as collateral. →
Risk reversal (25RR)
Implied vol of the 25-delta call minus that of the 25-delta put. →
Risk-neutral probability
The probabilities that make expected discounted payoffs match market prices. →
RPV01
Risky annuity: the PV of receiving 1 per year until maturity or default, whichever comes first. →
Seasonality
The regular monthly pattern in price indices that the forward index curve must include. →
Series
A version of an index with a fixed list of names, launched every March and September. →
Shifted lognormal
Black's model applied to rate + shift, so it still works when rates are near or below zero. →
SimpleFlow
Booking record for a single known cash payment; PV = ± amount × discount factor. →
SOFR / SONIA / SARON / €STR
Overnight risk-free reference rates in USD, GBP, CHF and EUR. →
Special
A bond in high demand to borrow, whose repo rate falls below GC. →
Spot date
The standard settlement date for FX, usually two business days after the trade. →
STIR future
Short-term interest rate future quoted as 100 minus the rate for a future three-month (or one-month) period. →
Straddle
A call plus a put at the same strike. It pays on a move in either direction. →
Strike
The fixed price at which an option can be exercised. →
Strike date
The date on which an FVA's straddle strike is set at the then-forward. →
Structured product
A note combining a bond with options or other derivatives into one security with a tailored payoff. →
Survival probability
Q(t) = e−λt, the chance the company has not defaulted by time t. →
Swap spread
Swap rate minus the government bond yield of the same maturity. →
TBA
To-be-announced: a forward trade on agency pools where the exact pools are named two days before settlement. →
Tick value
Money value of a one-basis-point price move for one contract ($25 for 3M SOFR). →
Time value
Premium minus intrinsic value: the value of the chance that the option ends further in the money. →
Total return swap (TRS)
Swap where one side receives an asset's income and price change and pays a floating funding rate plus spread. →
Upfront
Cash paid at the start of a fixed-coupon CDS to settle the difference between the coupon and the market spread. →
Variance swap
A swap paying Nvar × (realised variance − strike²). →
Variation margin
Cash paid or received each evening equal to the change in the futures settlement price times multiplier times lots. →
Vega notional
Money per vol point; for a variance swap, Nvar = Nvega/(2K). →
Version
Counter that increases each time a constituent of the series defaults. →
Vol cube
Swaption implied vols by expiry, tenor and strike. →
Volatility swap
A swap paying Nvega × (realised vol − strike). →
WAC
Weighted-average coupon: the average mortgage rate paid by the borrowers in a pool. →
Warrant
A company-issued right to buy new shares at a fixed strike before expiry. →
Weekly option
A listed option expiring on a given weekday within the month, rather than at the monthly expiry. →
Yield to maturity
The single discount rate that makes a bond's cashflows equal its dirty price. →
YoY caplet / floorlet
An option paying max(YoY − K, 0) or max(K − YoY, 0) on one year's inflation. →
YoY inflation
Year-on-year inflation: the index this year divided by the index a year earlier, minus 1. →
Z-spread
Constant spread over the zero curve that reprices a bond to its market price. →
Zero rate
The rate for money locked up from today to one date with no payments in between. →
